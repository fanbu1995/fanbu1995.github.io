    <p class="section-label">Overview</p>
    <h2>Statistical methodology motivated by modern scientific data</h2>
    <p>My research develops principled statistical methods for settings where the data are incomplete, high-dimensional, distributed, or observed through complex scientific mechanisms. Many of these problems arise in infectious disease studies and large-scale observational healthcare research.</p>
    <p>You can also find a broader list of publications on <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_WI7hMUAAAAJ&hl=en">Google Scholar</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card-grid">
    <article class="info-card">
      <h3>Infectious disease models</h3>
      <p>Likelihood-based and Bayesian inference for partially observed epidemic processes, social contact dynamics, and transmission structure.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="info-card">
      <h3>Bayesian computation</h3>
      <p>Flexible computational tools for complex latent-variable models, especially where mechanistic structure and uncertainty quantification matter.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="info-card">
      <h3>Real-world evidence</h3>
      <p>Bias-aware evidence synthesis and large network studies using observational health data across multiple institutions and databases.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
